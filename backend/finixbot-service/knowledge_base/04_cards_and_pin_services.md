# Debit and Credit Card Services

Finix Bank provides instant virtual card generation and physical card management linked to your bank accounts.

## Card Issuance
- **Debit Cards**: Linked directly to your SAVINGS or CURRENT account for ATM withdrawals and POS/online transactions.
- **Credit Cards**: Offered to eligible account holders with pre-approved credit limits.

## How to Issue a New Card
1. Navigate to **Cards** from your customer dashboard.
2. Select your linked account type (SAVINGS or CURRENT).
3. Choose Card Type (DEBIT CARD or CREDIT CARD).
4. Click **Confirm Card Issuance**. Your card details will immediately appear in your Active Cards wallet.

## Resetting ATM PIN
- On any active card tile, click **Reset PIN**.
- Enter your new 4-digit security PIN.
- The PIN is instantly updated on Finix Bank's core banking server.
