# Opening a Bank Account with Finix Bank

Finix Bank offers paperless, instant digital account opening for individual retail customers and business enterprises.

## Account Types Available

### 1. Savings Account
- **Minimum Balance**: ₹1,000 for regular savings accounts.
- **Interest Rate**: 4.0% p.a. calculated daily and credited quarterly.
- **Features**: Free virtual debit card, unlimited online transfers, free mobile banking, zero maintenance fee for active accounts.

### 2. Current Account
- **Minimum Balance**: ₹10,000.
- **Features**: Designed for business entities, sole proprietors, and merchants. Offers high daily transaction limits, free overdraft facilities, and priority branch service.

## Eligibility & Documents Required
- Must be a resident of India aged 18 years or above.
- Aadhaar Card (12-digit UIDAI number) with mobile linking.
- PAN Card (10-character Permanent Account Number).
- Portrait selfie / photograph for facial identity matching.

## How to Apply Online
1. Click **Open Account** on the Finix Bank portal.
2. Select your role (**CUSTOMER**) and enter your personal details (Full Name, DOB, Mobile Number, Email, Address, Aadhaar, and PAN).
3. Set your secure account password and log in to your new portal dashboard.
