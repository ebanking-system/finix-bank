# Finix Bank Loan Products & EMI Guidelines

Finix Bank provides flexible, low-interest retail loans with instant digital evaluation.

## Available Loan Products

### 1. Personal Loan
- **Interest Rate**: 10.5% p.a. onwards.
- **Loan Amount**: ₹10,000 to ₹10,00,000.
- **Tenure**: 6 to 60 Months.
- **Purpose**: Medical emergencies, travel, home renovation, or weddings.

### 2. Home Loan
- **Interest Rate**: 8.5% p.a.
- **Loan Amount**: ₹5,00,000 to ₹1,00,00,000.
- **Tenure**: 12 to 240 Months.
- **Purpose**: New residential property purchase or construction.

### 3. Vehicle / Auto Loan
- **Interest Rate**: 9.25% p.a.
- **Loan Amount**: ₹50,000 to ₹25,00,000.
- **Tenure**: 12 to 84 Months.
- **Purpose**: Two-wheeler and four-wheeler financing.

## Application & Approval Workflow
1. Navigate to **Loans & EMIs** from your customer dashboard.
2. Select your desired Loan Type from the dropdown catalog.
3. Enter requested Loan Amount and Tenure (in months).
4. Submit application for employee credit audit.
5. Once approved and disbursed by bank staff, funds are directly credited to your active account balance.

## EMI Payments & Repayment Schedules
- Every disbursed loan generates a monthly repayment schedule with fixed EMI due dates.
- Customers can view full due dates, amounts due, and payment status in the **Repayment Schedule** modal.
- To pay an EMI, click **Pay EMI**, choose your payment source account (SAVINGS or CURRENT), and confirm instant debit.
