# Money Transfers and Beneficiary Management

Finix Bank supports instant 24/7 intra-bank and inter-bank electronic fund transfers.

## Adding Beneficiaries
1. Go to **Beneficiaries** in your customer portal.
2. Click **Add Beneficiary**.
3. Enter Beneficiary Account Number, IFSC Code, Account Type, and Nickname.
4. Beneficiaries are saved for 1-click quick transfers.

## Transferring Funds
- Select source account (SAVINGS or CURRENT).
- Enter target account number or select a saved beneficiary.
- Enter transfer amount and optional remark/description.
- Transfers are processed in real-time with automatic CREDIT and DEBIT transaction logs.

## Transaction Filters & History
- View complete statement under **Transactions**.
- Filter history by date range, account number, status (SUCCESS / FAILED), and nature (CREDIT / DEBIT).
