# Fixed Deposit (FD) Investment Guide

Finix Bank offers high-yield, guaranteed-return term deposits.

## Tenure & Interest Rate Structure
- **1 Year (ONE_YEAR)**: 6.50% p.a.
- **2 Years (TWO_YEARS)**: 7.00% p.a.
- **3 Years (THREE_YEARS)**: 7.25% p.a.
- **4 Years (FOUR_YEARS)**: 7.50% p.a.
- **5 Years (FIVE_YEARS)**: 7.75% p.a.

## Deposit Limits & Booking
- **Minimum Deposit**: ₹1,000.
- Funds are directly debited from your selected SAVINGS or CURRENT account upon deposit confirmation.
- Deposits are DICGC insured up to ₹5,00,000 per customer.

## How to Book an FD Online
1. Open **Fixed Deposits** in your dashboard.
2. Choose source account (SAVINGS or CURRENT).
3. Enter deposit amount (minimum ₹1,000).
4. Select tenure (1 to 5 years).
5. Click **Confirm & Open Fixed Deposit**.
