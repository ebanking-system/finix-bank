# Digital KYC Verification Guidelines

Know Your Customer (KYC) compliance is mandatory for all active bank accounts under RBI regulations.

## Required Documents
- **Aadhaar Card**: 12-digit UIDAI number / PDF or scanned image.
- **PAN Card**: 10-character alphanumeric PAN card / PDF or scanned image.
- **Selfie Photo**: Direct portrait image for facial identification matching.

## How to Submit KYC Online
1. Open the **Digital KYC** tab in your customer dashboard.
2. Choose your submission mode:
   - **Direct File Upload**: Upload PDF or image files of Aadhaar card, PAN card, and selfie photo.
   - **Number & Image URL Entry**: Provide Aadhaar number, PAN number, and portrait image URL.
3. Click **Submit KYC For Approval**.

## Audit & Verification Timeline
- Submitted KYC applications enter the bank employee audit queue.
- Bank employees review uploaded identity documents within 24 to 48 business hours.
- Upon approval, full account privileges (high transfer limits, loan eligibility, card issuance) are automatically unlocked.
