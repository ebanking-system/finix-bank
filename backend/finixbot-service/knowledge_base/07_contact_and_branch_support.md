# Customer Support & Branch Hours

Finix Bank is dedicated to round-the-clock customer satisfaction and banking security.

## Customer Support Channels
- **Toll-Free Customer Care**: 1800-FINIX-BANK (1800-34649-2265)
- **Email Support**: support@finixbank.com
- **Live Assistant**: FinixBot AI Chat Assistant (available 24/7 in bottom-right corner of portal)

## Branch Working Hours
- **Monday to Friday**: 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM IST.
- **1st, 3rd, and 5th Saturdays**: 9:30 AM to 1:00 PM IST.
- **2nd and 4th Saturdays**: Closed (Bank Holiday).
- **Sundays & National Holidays**: Closed.

## Security Alert Notice
- Finix Bank staff will NEVER ask for your password, PIN, or OTP over phone call or SMS.
- Always log in through the official domain (`http://localhost:5173` or `https://finixbank.com`).
